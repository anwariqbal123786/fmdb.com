Key observations

  • Vendor-led network. Vendors maintain 34,847 km, 76% of the 45,648 km network. RIO3 (97%) and RIO2 (93%) are almost fully outsourced.
  • RIO1 is the in-house core. It holds 6,689 km of the 10,801 km SComm maintains (62%) and 41% of all telco sites, with about 2.5 km of network per site against 5 to 10 km in the other RIOs.
  • Concentration. RIO2 has the longest network at 13,465 km, led by Sylhet at 4,105 km (9% of the total), followed by Rangpur and Mymensingh.
  • High-capacity backbone. 1,291 major links, of which 439 (34%) are above 200G. RIO1 and RIO2 carry 806 of them (62%).
  • UG coverage is thin. UG is 17.7% of the network overall, from 11.9% in RIO2 to 24.4% in RIO3.
  • Span of control. Sarker Shahin Ahmed heads both Banani and Mirpur Sub-Centers in RIO1.

Source: Sub-Center Head with Network Details. Total Network = SComm + vendor maintained km. UG is read as a part of Total Network. POPs exclude IF3 Union. Contact, email and address fields are not shown.
